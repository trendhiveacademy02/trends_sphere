Research Overview

The rapid adoption of digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay) has shifted the fraud landscape from traditional card-not-present (CNP) attacks to sophisticated "enrollment abuse" vectors.

This research module dissects the provisioning protocols used by major issuers, highlighting weaknesses in Yellow Path / Green Path verification logic. We analyze how threat actors exploit BIN ranges with weak 3DS configurations to bypass OTP challenges.

Critical Risk Vectors

Device Spoofing

Analysis of device fingerprint emulation tools used to mimic trusted devices during wallet provisioning.

BIN Attacks

Understanding brute-force attacks on specific BIN ranges with static CVC/Expiry configurations.

IVR Bypass

Case studies on social engineering tactics targeting Interactive Voice Response verification systems.

Tokenization Flaws

Technical breakdown of how TSP (Token Service Provider) trust scores are calculated and manipulated.
